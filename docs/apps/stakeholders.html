Questions to ask before deciding

  • Who bears the cost of this choice, and did they have a say in it?
  • If it hurts someone, can we undo it, and how fast?
  • Would we be comfortable explaining it to each group face to face?
  • What do we owe the people who carry the risk: time, money, or simply a clear explanation?
  • Does it still look right a year from now, not just this quarter?
