Frame Studio · the vision

Planning at
the frontier

Keep the arithmetic boring. Make everything around it extraordinary.

Frame Studio is a working sketch of what planning and decision making could feel like when a defensible, deterministic engine stays at the centre, and the experience around it is lit up, imaginative, and yours to explore.

The idea

Two promises that usually pull apart

The core

Defensible

  • The same inputs give the same numbers, every time.
  • Every number traces back to the blocks and rows that made it.
  • Access rights decide what each person can see and change.
  • A change recalculates only what depends on it.
The surface

Alive

  • Pages that explain themselves instead of waiting to be read.
  • Formulas you can watch, models you can walk through.
  • Many ways in, all leading to the same governed numbers.
  • A plan that feels less like a spreadsheet and more like a story you can steer.

Most tools ask you to choose: trustworthy and rigid, or delightful and loose. The bet here is that you don’t have to. Pigment’s calculation engine stays the single source of truth. Everything built on top is free to be playful, because nothing up there can bend the math.

Prologue

The engine stays the engine

At the centre sits Graphite, the calculation engine. It stores only the cells that hold data, recalculates only what a change touches, and applies access rights before anything reaches a screen. It is meant to be dull in the best way: predictable, explainable, the same for everyone.

Every door that follows reads from it. None of them can change how it adds up.

Principles

What the experiment holds to

  1. Determinism is the floor, not the ceiling.The engine guarantees the numbers. That guarantee is what frees everything above it to experiment.
  2. Every surface is a door, not a fork.Dashboards, 3D, first person, conversation, agents: all read the same governed truth. None keeps a private copy.
  3. Show the math.Any number should be one gesture away from its formula, its blocks and its source rows.
  4. Approval before write.People and agents can propose anything. Only an approved change reaches the model.
  5. Play is a feature.Curiosity is how people learn a model. An experience that invites it is not a toy.
  6. Explorable by someone who didn’t build it.The test of a good model is whether a newcomer can find their way around it alone.
  7. The world is the final check.Plans inform decisions. Reality decides, and the plan learns from it.
  8. Sample to start, live to decide.Everything here runs on sample data to explore freely. In Pigment, the same code runs on the real thing.
Further out

Where this could go

Speculative, on purpose. None of this is built yet; all of it keeps the same rule: a deterministic core, and as much imagination around it as people can use.

Planning rooms

Finance, sales and operations stand inside the same model at the same time, each seeing what their access allows, arguing over one set of numbers instead of three.

Scenario worlds

Each scenario becomes a parallel version of the world you can step between. Walk the downside, then the upside, and feel the difference before you choose.

Agents on the night shift

Heavy analysis runs overnight. In the morning there is a brief, and every claim in it links to the cells that support it. No receipts, no claim.

Pages that reshape to the question

Ask why a region is short and the frame rearranges itself to answer: the bridge, the culprit products, the shelf. Then it folds back.

Branching reviews

The board meeting becomes a choose-your-own-adventure. Pick a path, the model follows, and every branch can be replayed exactly.

Plans that remember the field

What happened on the shelf, in the call, at the customer, flows back as structured learning the next plan starts from.

Learning by playing

New planners learn the model the way people learn a city: by walking it, getting a little lost, and finding the landmarks.

Explain anything

Point at any number on any surface and the path behind it lights up, from the page to the formula to the source system.

Many hands, one truth

Humans in Pigment, agents through MCP, apps on the edge: all reading and proposing against the same definitions and the same governance.

The engine is the promise. The experience is the invitation.
